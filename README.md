# Going Concern

**An open book about the KPMG Australia audit scandal.**

In May 2026, a whistleblower's complaint inside KPMG Australia — alleging that audit partners had misused confidential board papers of audit client Lendlease — began to unravel. Within weeks the firm's CEO and audit head had resigned, its chairman was under fire, ASIC had opened a formal investigation, governments began freezing hundreds of millions of dollars in contracts, and the firm's defence unit was put on the block for $1.

*Going Concern* tells that story as it unfolds. The title is borrowed from the audit opinion every auditor must eventually render on every client: can this entity keep operating? Here the question is turned on the auditor itself.

## A note on sources

This book is built on public reporting — chiefly *The Australian*, AFR, SMH/The Age, ABC, Reuters and parliamentary records — plus whistleblowers' allegations made under parliamentary privilege. Chapter notes link to the underlying articles.

Archived copies of paywalled source articles and the raw article corpus are deliberately **not** included in this repository; they are third-party copyright. Only original writing, research summaries, and links are published here.
